SOAP is a simple way to study one passage at a time. Each letter is one step: Scripture, Observation, Application and Prayer. All you need is a Bible, a pen and a page.

## S: Scripture

Read the passage slowly, then copy one verse that stands out to you onto your page.

> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.
>
> Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path. (Psalm 119:105, KJV and WEB)

## O: Observation

Write what you notice. Who is speaking? What words repeat? What does the verse say about God?

## A: Application

Write one way the verse speaks to your life today. Keep it specific and small.

## P: Prayer

Close with a short written prayer that uses what you learned.
